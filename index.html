KickIt Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

In short. KickIt counts your football juggling with your phone's camera. The counting happens on your phone. During the beta, every session is recorded and sent to the KickIt team so we can check and improve the count. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics. You can delete everything from inside the app.

Who we are

KickIt is made by Dwane Debono and Paul Zammit. Dwane Debono publishes the Android app on Google Play. Paul Zammit publishes the iPhone app. Both of us look after your data together, and either of us can answer your questions.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the KickIt app on Android and iPhone during the closed beta, and the website kickit.site. KickIt is a working name for the beta. When the app gets its final name, a new policy will replace this one.

What stays on your phone

These never leave your phone:

  • The name you give yourself in the app.
  • Your history, stats and the leaderboard, including its pictures.
  • Your settings.

The app doesn't use your microphone, your location, your contacts, or any account.

What we collect during the beta

The beta records each session and uploads it to our server. Before the first session, the app asks you to agree to this. You can't use the beta without agreeing, because checking the count is what the beta is for.

WhatDetails
Session videoVideo from the back camera while you juggle, with no sound. It shows whoever is in front of the camera, which may include other people in the background.
Count and runsHow many touches the app counted, when each run started and ended, and why it ended.
Motion dataThe phone's motion sensors during a session, so we can tell if the phone moved.
Phone performanceBattery level, temperature, processor load and frame timings during a session, plus the phone model and Android or iOS version.
Reports you sendWhen you report a wrong count: what went wrong, the right count if you give it, and any note you write. Sending a report is up to you.
Device idA short id that groups your uploads, made from your phone model and a one-way hash of an id Android gives the app. It doesn't identify you by name and isn't an advertising id.

Like any internet service, our network providers see your IP address while your phone uploads. We don't use it to locate you.

Why we use it

  • To check whether the app counted your juggling correctly.
  • To find and fix problems, such as runs that end too early or phones that get too hot.
  • To improve the counting. We mark touches in some videos by hand and use them to train and test the models that run on your phone.
  • To let you delete your data from the app.

We never use your data for advertising. We never sell it, and we never share it with anyone else for their own use.

If you are in the EU, the UK, or a country with similar laws, our legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time, as described under "Deleting your data".

Who can see it

Only the two people who make KickIt, Dwane Debono and Paul Zammit, can see your videos and session data.

Two companies help us run the server. Neither may use your data for anything else:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH hosts our server in [COUNTRY: confirm the server's location with Paul].
  • Cloudflare, Inc. carries the traffic between your phone and our server. Your phone encrypts session files before sending them, so Cloudflare can't read them.

How we protect it

  • Uploads use HTTPS. Each piece of a session is also encrypted on your phone with AES-256 before it is sent.
  • Uploads only happen on Wi-Fi.
  • Only the KickIt team can access the server.

Videos also stay on your phone after they upload only if you turn on "Keep recordings on this phone". Otherwise the app removes each file from your phone once our server has it.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to You, then Privacy and my data, then Delete my data. This deletes every session your phone has sent us. Then it clears the recordings, history and leaderboard on your phone.

Deleted sessions are removed from everything we use at once. They are erased for good after 7 days. Until then, we can only restore them if you ask us to. Copies in our server backups are erased within [BACKUP PERIOD].

Uninstalling the app removes everything on your phone, but not what we already hold. To delete that too, use Delete my data before you uninstall, or email us.
